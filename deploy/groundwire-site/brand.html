GroundWire logo concepts

Three directions built on one idea: AI signals converging into a node and wired to ground, meaning reliable, evidence-based AI.

Grounded Node Recommended

Inputs converge into an AI node whose wire runs to the electrical ground symbol. Reads clearly at favicon size. Used on the site.

G-Trace

A "G" drawn as a circuit trace ending in a terminal node, with a small ground mark. Monogram-style; works well as an app icon.

Hex Ground

A hexagonal network cell with the ground symbol inside. More "infrastructure" in feel; suits a platform or product brand.
