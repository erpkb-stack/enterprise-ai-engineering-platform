# Refund and Warranty Policy (DEMO DATA)

Customers may request a full refund within 30 days of purchase for any unused product.
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days.
Digital subscriptions can be cancelled at any time and are refunded on a prorated basis for annual plans.

ACME hardware products include a 2 year limited warranty covering manufacturing defects.
The warranty does not cover accidental damage, water damage, or unauthorized repairs.
Warranty claims require proof of purchase and are processed through the support portal.
